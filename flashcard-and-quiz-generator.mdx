Turns your own notes into flashcards and a multiple-choice quiz. Each card and question links back to the line in your notes it came from, so you can check it in one click.

## Overview

A study app that runs locally on your machine. You paste notes, type them in, or upload TXT, Markdown, PDF or DOCX files. The app then builds two things from them:

- **A flashcard deck** with spaced-repetition review. You rate each card Again, Hard, Good or Easy, and the app schedules the next review.
- **A multiple-choice quiz** with four options per question, an explanation for the right answer and a short note on each wrong one.

Each card and question includes a short quote from your notes. Click "Show source" to open your notes with that quote highlighted. If the app can't find the quote in your notes, it flags the item so you know to double-check it.
